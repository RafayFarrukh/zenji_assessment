An anime-inspired streetwear storefront for ZENJI, built as a frontend with
Next.js 15 (App Router), TypeScript strict and Tailwind v4.

Ten pieces, limited stock, no restocks. The full shop flow works end to end:
browse the drop, open a product, pick a size, add to cart, check out, and get
a confirmed order with an order number.

Also included: lookbook, our story, FAQ with a size guide, 404, client-side
search, per-page SEO metadata, sitemap, robots and Product JSON-LD.

No backend, database, auth or payment integration, as briefed. Products are
typed data; the cart lives in localStorage; no card details are requested.

Notes on quality are in the repository README.
